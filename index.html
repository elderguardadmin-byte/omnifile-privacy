OmniFile Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

OmniFile is a file manager with tools for converting and compressing files on your phone or tablet. It is made by Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

OmniFile does its work on your device. We do not run a server for it, there are no accounts, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your files or any information about you. The app uses the internet only to fetch a file from a link you give it; nothing is sent to us.

Your files

  • You choose which files and folders OmniFile can use: through your system's file, folder or photo picker, by sharing or opening a file in OmniFile from another app, or by giving it a link to a file.
  • OmniFile sees the files in its own library and in the folders you add. On iPhone and iPad it also has its own OmniFile folder, which the Files app shows under On My iPhone or On My iPad. If you turn on "See all your files" on Android, it can see the files in your device's shared storage and on SD cards and USB drives you connect; Android still keeps other apps' private folders (Android/data and Android/obb) closed. It never sends any of these files anywhere.
  • You can remove a folder from OmniFile at any time, and on Android you can turn "See all your files" off in Android's settings. OmniFile keeps working with the folders you added.
  • If a folder you add belongs to a cloud service (for example iCloud Drive), that service may download or sync the files you open or change there, under its own terms. OmniFile itself sends nothing to it or to us.
  • OmniFile makes its own copy of each file you bring into its library, and of a file in your folders when you use a tool on it. It keeps these copies in the app's private storage on your device, together with the results you create, a short history of what you did (for example "converted to PNG"), and the lists it needs to work, such as the folders you added, your favourites and your recent files.
  • Tools (converting, compressing and the others) work on OmniFile's own copy and never change your original. The result is kept in OmniFile's library and, when you start from one of your folders, can also be saved there as a new file next to the original.
  • File actions change your real files because you ask them to: renaming, moving, copying, deleting, and saving changes in OmniFile's text editor. A flow you set up does only the steps you chose for it, which can include renaming or moving originals or putting them in Trash.
  • When you delete files in OmniFile, they go to its Trash first, and you can put them back. Files from your folders wait in a hidden folder named .OmniFileTrash in the same place. Once a file has been in Trash for 30 days, OmniFile deletes it for good the next time it is opened; you can also empty Trash sooner. Where a place has no Trash (for example a drive that cannot be written to), OmniFile asks before it deletes for good.
  • OmniFile's library, results, history and lists are not uploaded anywhere. They are also left out of your device's cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, so they stay on this device only. Files in your own folders are backed up, or not, as your device already does. On iPhone and iPad, the OmniFile folder is ordinary app storage, and your settings and the tools you used recently are kept in the app's preferences (tool names only, never file names); both are part of your device's own backups.
  • When you save, share, print or open a file in another app, it goes where you choose, through your system's save dialog, share sheet, print dialog or list of apps. (Printing is on iPhone and iPad.) Other apps' file pickers can show OmniFile's library on Android and the OmniFile folder on iPhone and iPad; an app gets only the files you pick there.
  • If you save OmniFile's settings to a file, that file holds your settings, presets, flows, favourites and the names of the folders you added, never the contents of your files or any password, and it goes where you choose.
  • You can delete files and results inside the app and clear the history. Uninstalling OmniFile removes its library and, on iPhone and iPad, the OmniFile folder. It does not remove files in your other folders. Files you deleted in OmniFile in the last 30 days stay in a hidden Trash folder in those folders until you empty Trash, so empty Trash before you remove the app.

Fetching a file from a link

If you paste or share a link to a file, your device downloads that file directly from the server in the link, and from any server that link sends it on to. Each of those servers sees what any website you visit sees: your device's internet address, the link itself, and the name of the app asking for it. We are not part of that connection and receive nothing.

  • OmniFile fetches only secure (https) links, and refuses links to addresses on your own network or device.
  • It sends no cookies, passwords or sign-in details, and keeps none.
  • If the site sends it on to another address, OmniFile checks that address the same way, at most five times.
  • It saves the result only if it is a file, not a web page, and only up to a size limit (64 MiB).
  • It does not keep the link. The file's history records only the website's name (for example example.com), not the rest of the address.
  • When you fetch a link, your device may also contact the certificate authority's revocation server for that site. Nothing is sent to us. This is a standard check that the site's security certificate has not been withdrawn. That server belongs to the certificate authority named in the site's certificate, not to us.

OmniFile makes no other internet connection: no update checks, no statistics, no advertising, no connection to us. (On Android, any connection for Google Play's installer check, described under Purchases, is made by the Google Play Store app, not by OmniFile.)

Camera

  • On iPhone and iPad, "Scan to PDF" uses the camera through Apple's document scanner, only while you are scanning. iOS asks your permission the first time. The pictures stay on your device and become a PDF in the app.
  • On Android, "Photos to PDF" opens your phone's own camera app to take each picture; OmniFile itself has no camera permission. The pictures stay on your device and become a PDF in the app.
  • OmniFile does not use the microphone.

Text recognition

When you make a searchable PDF from pictures of pages, the text on them is recognised on your device: on iPhone and iPad by Apple's built-in Vision framework, on Android by a recognition engine and language files bundled inside the app. OmniFile downloads no language data. No page, picture or text leaves your device.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices installed on your device. On Android it uses only voices that work without the internet, so the text is never sent to an online voice. On iPhone and iPad it uses Apple's voices on the device.

Notifications

If you allow them, OmniFile shows notifications on your device about work that is still running, such as file operations, conversions and flows, or that needs you. They are made on your device. OmniFile uses no push notification service, and no message comes from us.

What we never collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location. OmniFile can remove location data from your photos; it never asks for your own location.
  • No contacts, and none of your photos or files: the ones OmniFile can see or use stay on your device.
  • No analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or device identifiers.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

OmniFile contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software.

Purchases

OmniFile is bought once, through Google Play or the App Store. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The store handles the payment and its own records under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

On Android, Google Play adds an installer check to the copy of OmniFile it delivers, to confirm that the app was bought or installed through Google Play. When you open OmniFile, the check asks the Google Play Store app on your device. It works without an internet connection; now and then, if the Play Store app has been offline for a long time, the Play Store app needs to reach Google. That exchange is between your device and Google, under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us. If the check fails, you are asked to get the app from Google Play.

Children

OmniFile is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how OmniFile handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the release notes.
